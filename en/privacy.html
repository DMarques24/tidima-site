Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

In short
  • Health app data (heart rate, sleep, HRV, Apple Watch workout metrics) never leaves your iPhone.
  • Your account stores your profile, the workouts you create in the app and the water you log, so you can get them back on another iPhone.
  • The morning check-in only goes to your account if you allow it, and you can withdraw that permission at any time.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools. We don't sell data.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app, at any time.

3. Health app data (HealthKit)

Only after you allow it, Tidima reads from the Health app: heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep analysis, workouts, active energy, distances (walking, running, cycling and swimming) and water.

Tidima writes to the Health app the workouts you do with the Apple Watch app and the water you log.

  • All calculations (recovery, strain, sleep, run analysis) happen on your iPhone.
  • Health app data, workouts imported from your Apple Watch, their metrics and any changes you make to them stay on your iPhone only. They are not sent to our servers or to iCloud.
  • We never use Health app data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and we never share it with third parties.
  • You can revoke access at any time: Health app → your profile → Apps → Tidima.

4. What we never do

  • We don't show ads or use third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • We don't sell or rent personal data.
  • We don't track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • We don't make automated decisions with legal effects about you.

5. Where data is stored and who we share it with

We only share data with the providers we need for the app to work. They process data on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Supabase, Inc.: database, accounts and profile photos. Servers located in London, United Kingdom.
  • Resend, Inc.: sending account confirmation and password reset emails.
  • Google or Apple, only if you choose to sign in with them. In that case, Google's or Apple's privacy policy also applies.

Account data is stored in the United Kingdom, a country the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection. Some of these providers are US companies. When data is transferred outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We may also disclose data where required by law, for example at the request of a court.

6. Security

  • All connections to our servers are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each user can only read and change their own data, enforced by rules in the database itself.
  • Profile photos are kept in private storage that only their owner can access.
  • Your password is stored by the authentication provider in a hashed, irreversible form; we never see it.
  • Your session is stored in the iPhone Keychain.

7. How long we keep data

  • While your account exists, we keep your account data.
  • When you delete a workout or water entry, it is marked as deleted on the server so it also disappears from your other devices. It is permanently erased when you delete your account.
  • When you withdraw consent for the morning check-in, we immediately delete the answers stored in your account. They remain on your iPhone.
  • When you delete your account, we immediately delete your profile, photo, workouts, water and check-ins. The provider's backups may keep them for a limited period until they are overwritten.
  • Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it or uninstall Tidima. If iPhone backups are turned on, they may include app data; those backups are controlled by you and Apple.
  • Technical logs are deleted automatically by the provider after a short period.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Delete your account and all its data, directly in the app: Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Delete account.
  • Correct your data: Profile → Edit profile, or by editing your workouts and entries.
  • Withdraw consent for the morning check-in: Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Privacy → "Save check-ins to my account". Withdrawing does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Access your data and receive it in a commonly used format (portability).
  • Restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interest.

For requests you can't make in the app, email suporte@tidima.com. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm that you own the account.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD, or with the authority in the country where you live.

9. Notifications and widgets

Workout and water reminders, feedback requests and the end-of-rest alert are local notifications created on your iPhone. We don't use a notification server.

Widgets, Live Activities and the Apple Watch app receive a summary of your data (for example today's water or your recovery) through a shared space on the device and the connection between your iPhone and watch.

10. Minimum age

Tidima is intended for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you know that a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version on this page with a new date. If the change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.
