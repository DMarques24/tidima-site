3. Health and safety

Recovery, strain, sleep need, water goal, estimated strength and run analysis are estimates, calculated from Apple Watch data and published formulas (for example Banister's TRIMP and the Epley and Tanaka formulas). They may not be accurate.

  • Don't make medical decisions based on the app.
  • Talk to a healthcare professional before starting an exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, are pregnant or are recovering from an injury.
  • Stop training and seek medical help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or other unusual symptoms.
  • You are responsible for how you train, the weights and intensities you choose and the equipment you use.

Logging habits such as alcohol intake is only meant to show you their effect on your recovery. It does not encourage drinking.
