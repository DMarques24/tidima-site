Frequently asked questions

Recovery says "Calibrating". Why?

Tidima compares each night with your personal average. It needs at least 4 nights of sleep with your Apple Watch.

No sleep data shows up.

Turn on sleep tracking on your Apple Watch (Health app → Sleep) and sleep with the watch on.

My Apple Watch workouts don't show up.

Check that Tidima can read workouts: Health app → your profile → Apps → Tidima. Then open the workout list and pull down to refresh.

Can I edit an Apple Watch workout?

Yes. Tap the workout and edit it like any other. The original record in the Health app is never changed, and your edits stay on your iPhone.

How is the water goal calculated?

By default it's 35 ml per kg of body weight. You can set a different amount in Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Water.

I didn't get the confirmation email.

Check your spam folder. Open the link on the iPhone where Tidima is installed.

Does my Health app data go online?

No. Heart rate, sleep, HRV and Apple Watch workout metrics always stay on your iPhone. Your account only stores your profile, the workouts you create in the app and the water you log.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, photo, workouts, water and check-ins stored in your account. To also remove the data on your iPhone, uninstall Tidima.
